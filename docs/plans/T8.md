## How
1. **Numbers** (all in `TUNE`, `?tune` folder 「မျက်မှန်」): `goggleFaceK 1.0` (face hit with goggles down counts as a body hit — instead of `faceK 1.5`) · `goggleSoakK .4` (sting / drown overlay for the player) · `thuraK .8` (all damage Thura takes). No drawback anywhere.
2. **`soak()`**: `const fk = o.face ? (k.goggles === 'down' ? TUNE.goggleFaceK : TUNE.faceK) : 1;` then `g = o.grit * fk * (k.toughK ?? 1)`. For the player, the drown / sting increments × `goggleSoakK` when down. Face hit with goggles down: the pop word is the flat 「ဖျန်း」 (not 「မျက်နှာ!」) and both lenses flash (emissive up for .15 s).
3. **Who wears them**
   - **Thura** (④ `i === 0`, `ROLES.B[0]`): cast.js `goggles()` on his bean's `ch`, **down** from the start, `toughK = TUNE.thuraK` — the toughest kid.
   - the **④ goggles kid** (`i === 1`, today `hat: 'goggles'` pushed up): swap the hat for cast.js `goggles()` and put them **down** in a fight.
   - **you**: none by default. `?goggles` gives you a pair (T6 will read `S.goggles` from the save instead — war.html can read the same localStorage key then). Town kids keep the hat version, always up (no effect).
4. **G (key + pad)**: plays the `goggles` one-shot (~.9 s, `STILL` so you can't fire), the cups swing at its beat (`thrown` at t .32 → `set(!up)`), `k.goggles` flips. The pad shows up / down.
5. **NPCs**: in a round, Thura and the goggles kid keep them down; at the water-pot stand / OUT they push them up (a small touch: `up` while resting, `down` when back in).

## Results (code, 2026-11-06)
**What landed** — `cast.js`: `export { goggles }` (one line). `yangon/war.js`: `TUNE.goggleFaceK 1 / goggleSoakK .4 / thuraK .8` + `?tune` folder 「မျက်မှန်」; `k.goggles` (`'down' | 'up' | null`), Thura's `k.toughK` (a getter on `TUNE.thuraK`, so `?tune` is live); `soak()` as planned — a shielded face hit is counted (`STATS.face`) but *lands* like a body hit: flat 「ဖျန်း」, `hit` sound, the small jolt, no hit-stop beat — and both lenses flash (emissive .4 → 2.6 for .15 s). The player's drown / sting increments ×`goggleSoakK` with goggles down (every hit, as the plan says). Thura (B0) + the goggles kid (B1) wear cast.js `goggles()` on their bean, down; they push them up while `state === 'rest'` or OUT and pull them down again in a fight (one flip per 2 s at most, never mid-shot); a new round puts them back down. You: none unless `?goggles` (or `?goggles=0` to force none), or `S.goggles` in the story save — war.js reads `localStorage['thingyan.v6']` (save.js `KEY`) so T6 only has to write `S.goggles = true`. G / the 「မျက်မှန်」 pad (war.html, after the three weapon pads, hidden without goggles; orange ring = down, dimmed while swinging) plays cast.js's `goggles` one-shot; nothing while OUT, wiping, filling at a tap or mid-throw; a held pad is let go.
**How the flip works** — importing cast.js patches `goggles` (.9 s) onto the shared `ChibiAnimator` (same `chibi.js?v=31` module as world.js, so every war kid has it — checked: `typeof me.c.anim._goggles === 'function'`). chibi.js's `STILL` list isn't exported, so war.js makes the swing still per wearer by overriding that animator's `speed` getter (0 while `one === 'goggles'`; ai.js already stops on `speed === 0`); `shootAt` / `press` / `wipe` refuse while `k.gog` is set. cast.js's `_goggles` swings the cups at t > .32; war.js mirrors it at .34 s (and sets `thrown`) so a kid off screen (`anim.skip`, no pose) or a cut-short swing still flips, never twice.
**Versions** — `war.js?v=12` (war.html) · `cast.js?v=12` (war.js, main.js) → `main.js?v=28` (boot.js) → `boot.js?v=29` (yangon.html) · sw.js `y16`. cast.html / camlab.js / carry-lab.html still import `cast.js?v=10` (stale before this task, left alone).

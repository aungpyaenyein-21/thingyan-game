## Shots (`talkShot`)
| kind | target | camera |
|---|---|---|
| `ots` (default) | the speaker's head, pulled ¼ towards the listener | behind the **listener's** shoulder (the side away from the line of action, so the 180° rule holds for the whole scene), dist = SL + 1.4, elev .14, speaker on the ⅓ line |
| `two` | the midpoint, chest height | perpendicular to SL on the scene's side, dist max(4, SL × 1.9), elev .25 |
| `close` | the speaker's face | in front of the speaker, dist 2.0, elev .08 |
| `wide` | the group's centroid | the scene's side, dist to fit everyone + 1 m, elev .4 |

- **Rules**: same speaker twice → hold · first line with 3+ people → `wide`, then `ots` · the scene's side (which side of the line of action the camera lives on) is picked once per scene from the first speaker / listener pair and kept · cut = glide .35 s when the box advances · after the last line `release()` (.5 s glide back to the follow camera)
- **Never inside a wall**: test the camera spot with world.js's `camCollide` (pull-in) — if it pulls in by > 30 %, try the other shoulder; still blocked → `two`
- **Listener** = the last other speaker, else the player (hero), else the nearest scene member

## Acting (`talk.js`)
- **Speaker**: turns to face the listener (.25 s ease), `anim.talking = true` while `hud.typing()`, plays `act` if given (else one default gesture per **3rd** line from a small pool by mood: neutral → `nod`/none, `?` in the text → `scratch`, `!` → `point`), `face` as story.js already does (emote fallback until T6.5's `anim.face`)
- **Listeners**: head + body ease towards the speaker, a `nod` every ~3 s (seeded, not in sync), `react: 'bang' | 'drop' | 'heart' | 'note'` → emote on that line
- **Skip-safe**: a tap finishes typing → mouth stops; a fast tap-through ends one-shots early (`anim.skip`), and `end()` always restores: talking off, heads free, `release()`

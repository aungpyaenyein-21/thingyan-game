## Shots (`talkShot`)
| kind | target | camera |
|---|---|---|
| `ots` (default) | the speaker's head, pulled ¼ towards the listener | behind the **listener's** shoulder (the side away from the line of action, so the 180° rule holds for the whole scene), dist = SL + 1.4, elev .14, speaker on the ⅓ line |
| `two` | the midpoint, chest height | perpendicular to SL on the scene's side, dist max(4, SL × 1.9), elev .25 |
| `close` | the speaker's face | in front of the speaker, dist 2.0, elev .08 |
| `wide` | the group's centroid | the scene's side, dist to fit everyone + 1 m, elev .4 |

- **Rules**: same speaker twice → hold · first line with 3+ people → `wide`, then `ots` · the scene's side (which side of the line of action the camera lives on) is picked once per scene from the first speaker / listener pair and kept · cut = glide .35 s when the box advances · after the last line `release()` (.5 s glide back to the follow camera)
- **Never inside a wall**: test the camera spot with world.js's `camCollide` (pull-in) — if it pulls in by > 30 %, try the other shoulder; still blocked → `two`
- **Listener** = the last other speaker, else the player (hero), else the nearest scene member

## Acting (`talk.js`)
- **Speaker**: turns to face the listener (.25 s ease), `anim.talking = true` while `hud.typing()`, plays `act` if given (else one default gesture per **3rd** line from a small pool by mood: neutral → `nod`/none, `?` in the text → `scratch`, `!` → `point`), `face` as story.js already does (emote fallback until T6.5's `anim.face`)
- **Listeners**: head + body ease towards the speaker, a `nod` every ~3 s (seeded, not in sync), `react: 'bang' | 'drop' | 'heart' | 'note'` → emote on that line
- **Skip-safe**: a tap finishes typing → mouth stops; a fast tap-through ends one-shots early (`anim.skip`), and `end()` always restores: talking off, heads free, `release()`

### What was built (and where it differs from the plan)
- **Binding corrections** followed: story.js passes `{ ...o, who: k }`; the director resolves the speaker with `who(opts.who)`; a shop `talk()` (no id) → speaker = `W.talkWith` unless the line's name is yours. The hold does not wait for the typewriter (story.js `hold()` is untouched; the box types at 28 clusters/s inside it; a tap finishes the line, the next advances). The 180° side is picked at a scene's first shot and kept. `world.js` turns the old `talk` context off while `W.talkDirector` is set; `?talk=0` gives the old behaviour (checked: the context comes on during a talk). Listeners use chibi.js's `chat = 'listen'` (only when their loop is `idle` / `sit`, so a peek or a pump-on-shoulder pose isn't folded away); `anim.talking` drives the mouth only.
- **Line rules**: `shot` if given · else the line after a cue → `wide` (once; voice lines don't use it up) · a scene's first line with 3+ people → `wide` · else `ots`. Same speaker again (and the camera still the director's) → hold. `voice` → no cut. Listener: `to` · last other speaker · nearest cast member. Your own lines frame you. One gesture every 3rd line when the line has no `act` (`?`/「လား」「လဲ」 → `scratch`, `!` → `point`, face `mad`/`pout` → `hips`, else `nod`; seated → `nod`); none under `zone.fast` (cuts are instant there too). `react` → emote on the listeners.
- **Cue boundaries**: main.js's cue wrapper calls `director.cue()`; when the box stays shut for a frame (an async cue: Phoe Kyaw's bike, May Thu walking in, 1-6's lift), the director lets go of the camera (a cue may run its own `focus`) and the next line cuts in again. `begin()` / `end()` ride on main.js's existing `story.play` wrapper.
- **Added beyond the plan** (each found from the screenshots):
  - *Cheat to camera*: the speaker faces the listener but is turned up to 1.3 rad towards the camera when it would see less than a ¾ face (a wide / two side-on, or a room whose open side is behind them — 1-1's Mum faces into the corner otherwise).
  - `ots` with the pair too close (the solved shoulder offset can't push the listener's big chibi head out of the speaker's way: < `otsMin` .7 of the way) → `two`; over `otsMax` 4.5 m → `close`.
  - In a room `two` / `close` / `wide` swing into the open half (±60° of az π/4 — rooms.js's walls are at −x and −z for every room) instead of being refused; an `ots` that can't → falls back. Room walls are checked as planes (camCollide doesn't know them).
  - `wide` / `two` swing towards the speaker's face (`wideYaw` .8, `twoYaw` .35); the wide is centred halfway to the speaker (`wideSpeaker` .5 — a far speaker ended up under the minimap) and capped at 10 m.
  - `boxLift` .22: every shot tilts down a little (the camera stays put) so faces sit above the dialogue box.
  - Fallback chain longer than planned: ots → other shoulder → two (both sides) → wide → close (both sides); wide → other side → two → close.
  - Talk cuts glide target, az, distance **and elevation** together (k = 3/.35 s); `releaseTalk()` glides back in ~.5 s to the az the follow camera had before the scene (or the room's az). Other `focus()` callers (1-6 lift, 1-7 bowl) are unchanged; the director only releases its own shot.
